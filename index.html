💡 Booking Rules

  • Slot Compatibility: Two-wheelers must park in Zone A (A-01 to A-06). Four-wheelers park in Zone B (B-01 to B-06).
  • Duplicate Rejection: A vehicle with an active booking cannot book a second slot simultaneously.
  • Occupied Protection: The database transaction validates availability before committing to prevent double-booking.
  • Release Flow: When the vehicle leaves, click "Release Slot" to free the bay and archive the session.
